About Me

I am Dr. Afshana Ashraf, a physiotherapist dedicated to helping individuals improve movement, reduce pain and regain functional independence through personalized physiotherapy and rehabilitation.


Every patient is assessed individually and provided with an appropriate treatment and exercise program according to their condition and functional goals.

Physiotherapy Services

🏠 Home Physiotherapy

One-to-one physiotherapy sessions at your home.

🧠 Neurological Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation for neurological conditions with a focus on mobility and functional recovery.

🦴 Orthopaedic Physiotherapy

Management and rehabilitation of musculoskeletal and orthopaedic conditions.

👶 Pediatric Physiotherapy

Individualized physiotherapy for children with developmental and movement difficulties.

💪 Exercise Therapy

Therapeutic exercises designed according to individual needs and functional goals.

🏃 Post-operative Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation following surgery to support safe functional recovery.
